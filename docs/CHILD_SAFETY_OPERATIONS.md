# Child safety launch checklist

Public standards: `community-regler.html#barnsakerhet`. Reuses the existing
CarCommunity community rules and contact mailbox for both apps. Driver report
UI/backend changes must be released before the new reporting instructions are
treated as available on every installed version.

Before Play certification, the owner must name the responsible individual,
confirm the mailbox is monitored, assign a backup and validate staff access to
Driver's queue. A published policy is not proof of operational staffing. Use the
existing Driver moderator/admin roles; do not grant broad CarCommunity privileges.

Operational procedure:

1. Check the report queue and safety mailbox regularly. Prioritize suspected
   child abuse immediately on awareness; escalate to the designated person.
2. Locate the reported target using its ID and server-supplied bounded preview.
   Do not ask a reporter to forward illegal material, download it to personal
   devices, copy it into logs/PRs or circulate it through ordinary support.
3. Use existing hide/remove actions to make content unavailable. Restrict the
   responsible account through the authorized administrator where warranted.
   A report, blocking and moderation are separate actions; test each.
4. Follow applicable law and obtain competent guidance on authority reporting,
   preservation and lawful evidence access. Keep only necessary access-controlled
   incident references; avoid reproducing prohibited material. A generic support
   email or a resolved queue item does not complete a legally required report.
5. Record the action and responsible actor in the existing moderation audit,
   verify content is unavailable, and review related reports for recurrence.
6. Rehearse the procedure with harmless fixtures. Confirm the named Console
   safety contact can explain prevention and escalation practices.

No automatic CSAM scanning or automatic law-enforcement reporting is claimed.
The owner supplies the human responsibility and Console certification.
